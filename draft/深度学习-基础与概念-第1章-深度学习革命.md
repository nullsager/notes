#ai #读书笔记 

Machine learning today is one of the most important, and fastest growing, fields
of technology. 

机器学习是当今最重要、发展最快的技术领域之一。

Applications of machine learning are becoming ubiquitous, and solutions learned from data are increasingly displacing traditional hand-crafted algorithms.

机器学习的应用遍及各个领域，基于数据学习的解决方案正逐步取代传统的手工设计算法。


> [!NOTE] 
> ubiquitous 普遍存在的，无所不在的


This has not only led to improved performance for existing technologies but
has opened the door to a vast range of new capabilities that would be inconceivable if new algorithms had to be designed explicitly by hand. 

这不仅提高了现有技术的性能，而且为大量新功能打开了大门，如果新的算法必须明确地手工设计，这些新功能是不可想象的。


> [!NOTE] 
> inconceivable 不可想象的

One particular branch of machine learning, known as deep learning, has emerged
as an exceptionally powerful and general-purpose framework for learning from data.

机器学习的一个特殊分支，被称为深度学习，已经成为一种非常强大的通用框架，用于从数据中学习。


> [!NOTE] 
> exceptionally 非常，及其 general-purpose 通用的

Deep learning is based on computational models called neural networks which were originally inspired by mechanisms of learning and information processing in the human brain. 

深度学习的理论基础是被称为神经网络 (neural network) 的计算模型，这些模型最初是受到人类大脑中学习和信息处理机制的启发而产生的。

The field of artificial intelligence, or AI, seeks to recreate the powerful capabilities of the brain in machines, and today the terms machine learning and AI are often used interchangeably. 

人工智能 (Artificial Intelligence，AI) 领域致力于在机器中重现人脑的强大能力，而今天 “机器学习”和“人工智能”这两个术语常常被交替使用。

> [!NOTE] 
> term 术语 interchangeably 交替地

Many of the AI systems in current use represent applications of machine learning which are designed to solve very specific and focused problems, and while these are extremely useful they fall far short of the tremendous breadth of capabilities of the human brain. 

目前使用的许多人工智能系统代表了机器学习的应用，这些应用旨在解决非常具体和集中的问题，尽管这些应用非常有用，但与人类大脑的巨大广度相比，它们还差得很远。


> [!NOTE] 
> tremendous breadth 巨大广度

This has led to the introduction of the term artificial general intelligence, or AGI, to describe the aspiration of building machines with this much greater flexibility. 

这导致了通用人工智能（AGI）这个术语的引入，用来描述制造具有更大灵活性的机器的愿望。


> [!NOTE] 
> aspiration 渴望，愿景

After many decades of steady progress, machine learning has now entered a phase of very rapid development. 

经过几十年的稳步发展，机器学习现在已经进入了一个非常快速的发展阶段。

Recently, massive deep learning systems called large language models have started to exhibit remarkable capabilities that have been described as the first indications of artificial general intelligence (Bubeck et al., 2023).

最近，被称为大型语言模型的大规模深度学习系统已经开始表现出非凡的能力，这被描述为人工通用智能的第一个迹象
